# Smart Hotel Booking Engine - Business Intelligence Requirements

## Executive Summary Dashboard

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
1. **Occupancy Rate**: Percentage of rooms occupied vs. available
2. **Average Daily Rate (ADR)**: Average revenue per occupied room
3. **Revenue Per Available Room (RevPAR)**: Total room revenue / total rooms
4. **Guest Satisfaction Score**: Based on service usage and repeat bookings
5. **Booking Conversion Rate**: Confirmed bookings vs. inquiries
